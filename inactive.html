inactive accounts

an incomplete graveyard of places i used to hang out on the internet. some of these are genuinely abandoned, some i still occasionally visit, and some are just in a weird state of limbo. here's why.

inactive

reddit

the whole Persona situation was enough to put me off reddit. and on top of that, i really don't want to go through the hassle of switching to an entirely different operating system just so i can use its VPN to access one (1) website. i like wasting time on the internet as much as the next person, but there's a limit. reddit is not worth reorganising my computer over.

i used to get a lot of my shitposting from there. these days, x has mostly filled that gap. make of that what you will.

bluesky

i don't have anything particularly dramatic against bluesky. it's just... too similar to x for me to see the point in maintaining both accounts. i don't really need a second microblogging site that does roughly the same thing as the one i'm already using. fewer feeds to check, fewer accounts to keep alive. simple as that.

plus, their userbase is very anti-ai.

tiktok

okay, technically i still use tiktok. just not on my public account. i have a private account for actually browsing the app, and i haven't uploaded a video to either account in ages. so there's not much reason for me to keep using the public one. the account still exists; it's just not doing anything particularly interesting.

roblox

i do still play roblox every now and then, but very rarely. the biggest obstacle is its absolutely miserable linux support. the community has literally had to make its own ways of running roblox on linux, because apparently supporting anything other than david roblox's beloved windows is too much to ask.

i'm not quitting forever or anything. it's just hard to be enthusiastic about a game when getting it to run on your computer is its own little side quest.

comicfury

this one's a combination of several things. first, i'm not particularly good at visual art, so making a webcomic isn't exactly my strong suit. second, i'm not especially into most of the webcomics on the site, so i don't really have much reason to stick around as a reader either.

third, the community is pretty opposed to AI-generated art, including partial use, which means even that route wouldn't really work for me there. and honestly, my creative interests have shifted much more towards coding and writing stories anyway.

if i ever do make a webcomic and want to share it, there are other places to put it. x exists. and, god forbid, i could even host it on beep8.xyz itself. imagine using my own website for something.

retroachievements

mostly, i just lost interest. i still play retro games occasionally, but i'm much more casual about it now. i don't really feel the need to turn every old game into a checklist of achievements.

also, stuff like the pokémon clover situation hasn't exactly made me more interested in coming back. from my perspective, they've become a bit too willing to ban games, and as a libertarian i'm not particularly enthusiastic about that approach. ultimately, though, the main reason is much less exciting: i stopped caring about the achievements.

websim

there's no big controversy here. i just gradually stopped using it. i might still open it every now and then to check notifications or see what's going on, but i'm not actively making things there anymore. sometimes a website doesn't do anything wrong; your brain just decides it's done with it.

scratch

pretty much the same story as websim. i used to spend time on scratch, but eventually my interests moved elsewhere. i might revisit my account and look through notifications every once in a while, but that's about it. these days, if i'm in the mood to code something, i'm generally doing it outside of scratch. like, on my github or something.

potentially inactive

esolangs.org

this one's conditional. there's a proposed AI ban from 2026, and whether i keep using esolangs.org depends on what happens with it. if the proposal is rejected, i'll probably continue using the site. if it passes, i don't really see myself sticking around. so for now, consider this account's future status a giant question mark.

formerly inactive

plot twist: not every account on this page is abandoned. these are accounts i either came back to or am seriously considering returning to.

twitter / x

back when i was using reddit regularly, i barely touched x. reddit was where i got most of my shitposting, so there wasn't much reason to use both. now that reddit is effectively off the menu for me, x has become the replacement. somehow, the account i barely used ended up being one of the ones i actually check. funny how that works.

wattpad (@SomeoneReturns)

i've been thinking about coming back to wattpad, especially because of Deer In The Headlights. i already unpublished the one story i had on there because, looking back, it was unbelievably cringe. so the account is basically a clean slate again, which is honestly kind of nice.

i haven't fully committed to posting there regularly just yet, but for once an old account actually has a reason to exist again. we'll see where that goes.
